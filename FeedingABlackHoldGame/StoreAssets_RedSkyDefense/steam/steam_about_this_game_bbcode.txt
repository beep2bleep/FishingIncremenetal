[h1]Red Sky Defense[/h1]

Hold the base. Bank the scrap. Survive the next wave.

Red Sky Defense is a wave-defense incremental. Keep your base standing through escalating enemy waves, earn scrap, and pick upgrades between rounds. Invest in a meta tree that improves core combat, survivability, economy, and unlocks additional defensive layers as you progress.

[h2]What You Do[/h2]
[list]
[*]Fight waves that scale up as you push deeper into a run
[*]Earn and spend scrap on between-wave upgrades and choices
[*]Use high-impact tools like nukes when your build supports them
[*]Unlock and strengthen deployable-style systems through meta progression
[*]Optimize builds across repeat sessions
[/list]

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Readable wave-defense loop with incremental progression
[*]Between-wave offers that change how each run feels
[*]Meta upgrades spanning offense, defense, economy, and unlock branches
[*]Single-player progression tuned for “one more wave” momentum
[/list]

[h2]Best For Players Who Enjoy[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tower defense and wave survival games
[*]Incremental and meta-upgrade-heavy progression
[*]Build crafting through offers and permanent trees
[*]Optimization-focused solo play
[/list]
