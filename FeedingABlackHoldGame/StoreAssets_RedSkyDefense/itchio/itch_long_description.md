# Red Sky Defense

Hold the base. Bank the scrap. Survive what comes next.

Red Sky Defense is a **wave-defense incremental**: waves escalate, your base takes real pressure, and you turn combat performance into scrap. Between waves you choose upgrades; over time you unlock meta nodes that widen your toolkit and push your ceiling.

## Why play
- Tight loop: fight → rewards → choices → stronger next push
- Between-wave offers keep runs from feeling identical
- Meta progression rewards long-term planning
- Good fit for “one more wave” sessions

## Core loop
1. Enter combat waves and protect the base.
2. Earn scrap from performance as waves progress.
3. Pick between-wave upgrades and tune your build.
4. Spend meta currency on permanent tree upgrades.
5. Return with a stronger framework for higher pressure.

## Features
- Wave-based defense with scaling threats
- Scrap economy and between-wave upgrade offers
- Meta upgrade tree (offense, defense, economy, unlock branches)
- Single-player incremental optimization
