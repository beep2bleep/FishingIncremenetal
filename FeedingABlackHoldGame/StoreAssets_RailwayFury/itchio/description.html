Railway Fury — Demo 0.1

One more dive. One more upgrade. One more shot at the boss.

Aim down a monster-filled railway, dodge attacks and fire waves, and collect gold and gems to make your next run stronger. Railway Fury combines mouse-aimed corridor shooting with permanent incremental upgrades.

The railway keeps moving and drains your health. Strafe to line up shots, weave around danger and collect loot before the tracks claim you. Between runs, spend your earnings on damage, health, armor, returning daggers and new powers.

Build your next breakthrough

  • Explore the upgrade tree and claim achievement rewards.
  • Use Magpie to double collected gold and gems, or Ghost to slip past enemy attention while bosses keep fighting.
  • Combine ready powers with Shield, Regen and Arrow Storm as you unlock them.
  • Toggle double speed when you want to push the pace. Your track runtime follows the game speed.

What is in the demo?

Play the first railway, including its minibosses and final boss, with illustrated environments, saved progression and permanent upgrades. Aim freely with the mouse as the railway carries you forward.

The demo ends after the first railway's final boss. You can replay Level 1 afterward. Crown upgrades, the three later levels and the final ending choices are reserved for the full game. Demo saves are separate from the full game.

Controls

  • Mouse: aim.
  • Left click: shoot. Hold the button to keep firing once hold-fire is unlocked.
  • A / D or Left / Right arrows: strafe. Forward movement is automatic.
  • Space: activate the highlighted ready power. Each press uses one power. You can also click a ready power's button.
  • Shift: toggle double speed on or off.
  • Esc: pause or resume. The pause menu also provides settings and a return to upgrades.
  • 1 / 2, after unlocking Auto Shoot: select hold-fire / auto-fire.

Play in your browser or download for Windows

Use a keyboard and mouse. The browser build requires WebGL 2.0. If it runs slowly, try the Windows download. Extract the entire Windows ZIP and run RailwayFuryDemo.exe with the PCK and DLL files beside it. Steam is not required.

Windows supports English, Spanish, German, Portuguese, French, Italian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Thai, Indonesian, Czech, Catalan and Vietnamese. The browser build supports the same list except Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Thai.
