One more dive. One more upgrade. One more shot at the boss.

Aim down a monster-filled railway, dodge attacks and fire waves, and collect gold and gems to make your next run stronger. Railway Fury combines aiming with permanent incremental upgrades. Your weapons fire automatically from the first run: just aim at enemies within range.

The railway keeps moving and drains your health. Strafe to line up shots, weave around danger and collect loot before the tracks claim you. Between runs, spend your earnings on damage, health, armor, returning daggers and new powers.

Build your next breakthrough

  • Explore the upgrade tree and claim achievement rewards.
  • Use Magpie to double collected gold and gems, or Ghost to slip past enemy attention while bosses keep fighting.
  • Combine ready powers with Shield, Regen and Arrow Storm as you unlock them.
  • Toggle double speed when you want to push the pace. Your track runtime follows the game speed.

What is in the demo?

Play the first railway, including its minibosses and final boss, with illustrated environments, saved progression and permanent upgrades. The current menu has one Railway Fury play entry: click it to head to your upgrades and start a run.

The demo ends after the first railway's final boss. You can replay Level 1 afterward. Crown upgrades, the three later levels and the final ending choices are reserved for the full game. The Steam demo and full game share progression and unlocks. The demo always stays on Level 1. Browser saves remain in this browser.

Powers

Unlock powers in the upgrade tree, then press Space to use the currently highlighted power. Each press activates one ready power. Every power lasts 10 seconds, followed by a 30-second recharge.

  1. Shield: reduces all incoming damage by 50%.
  2. Magpie: doubles collected gold and gems. Boss crowns are unaffected.
  3. Arrow Storm: fires an additional arrow with each shot, dealing triple damage to bleeding enemies.
  4. Regen: restores health over time.
  5. Ghost: melee enemies stop pursuing and fliers stop shooting. Bosses remain aware and keep attacking.

Controls

  • Mouse: aim. Weapons fire automatically from the start.
  • A / D or Left / Right arrows: strafe. Forward movement is automatic.
  • Space: use the currently highlighted power. Each press activates one ready power. You can also click a ready power's button.
  • Shift: toggle double speed on or off.
  • Esc: pause or resume. The pause menu also provides settings and a return to upgrades.
  • Controller: left stick to move, right stick to aim, left trigger or bumper for powers, left-stick click to toggle double speed, Menu / Options to pause. Firing is automatic.

Play in your browser or download for Windows

Use a keyboard and mouse, or a controller with virtual-cursor menus. The browser build requires WebGL 2.0. If it runs slowly, try the Windows download. Extract the entire Windows ZIP and run RailwayFuryDemo.exe with the PCK and DLL files beside it. Steam is not required.

Windows supports English, Spanish, German, Portuguese, French, Italian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Thai, Indonesian, Czech, Catalan and Vietnamese. The browser build supports the same list except Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Thai.
