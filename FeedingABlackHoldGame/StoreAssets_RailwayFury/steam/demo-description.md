# Railway Fury Demo

Short description:

Try the first railway and boss in Railway Fury, a first-person incremental action game. Aim and shoot, dodge fire waves, collect loot, and buy permanent upgrades between dives. Build a stronger loadout and conquer the demo’s final challenge.

About this demo:

Take your first dives into Railway Fury, a first-person incremental action game about pushing farther down a cursed railway. Aim your crossbow, strafe around enemies and fire waves, collect loot, and spend your earnings on permanent upgrades.

The demo includes the first railway and its boss. Build up damage, health, armor, and powers over repeated runs, and claim achievements for extra upgrade resources. The demo ends after the first boss; later levels and crown upgrades are reserved for the full game.

Move left and right, aim with the mouse, click to shoot, and press Space to activate your next ready power. Hold Shift for double speed when you are ready for the extra risk.

DEMO MODE

Demo progress uses a separate save from the full game. The full version expands the journey through four themed railways, a larger upgrade tree, tougher bosses, and two endings.
