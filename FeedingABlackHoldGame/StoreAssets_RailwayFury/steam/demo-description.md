# Railway Fury Demo

Short description:

Aim while your weapons fire automatically from your first run. Dodge hazards, collect loot and buy permanent upgrades. Defeat the first railway boss in this free demo.

About this demo:

Take your first dives into Railway Fury, a first-person incremental action game about pushing farther down a cursed railway. Your weapons fire automatically from the first run: just aim at enemies within range. Strafe around enemies and fire waves, collect loot, and spend your earnings on permanent upgrades.

The demo includes the first railway and its boss. Build up damage, health, armor, and powers over repeated runs, and claim achievements for extra upgrade resources. The demo ends after the first boss; later levels and crown upgrades are reserved for the full game.

Move left and right, aim with the mouse while your weapons fire automatically, and press Space to activate your next ready power. Press Shift to toggle double speed when you are ready for the extra risk.

DEMO MODE

The Steam demo and full game share progression and unlocks. The demo always stays on Level 1; browser saves remain in the browser. The full version expands the journey through four themed railways, a larger upgrade tree, tougher bosses, and two endings.

Controller: left stick to move, right stick to aim, left trigger or bumper for the next ready power, left-stick click to toggle double speed, and Menu / Options to pause. Shooting is automatic from the start. Controller menus use a virtual cursor.
