# Data Breach Inc.

Pilot a breach rig into hostile corporate infrastructure, mine the firewall for resources, crack daemon cores, and get out before the system decides you are part of the cleanup.

Data Breach Inc. is an arcade incremental built around short sorties and long-term upgrades. Each run gives you a chance to mine blocks, break defenses, pressure a daemon core, bank resources, and push a little deeper into the machine.

## What You Do

- Fly into the firewall
- Mine blocks and collect resources
- Fight defensive pressure and daemon cores
- Extract before the run turns against you
- Spend the haul on permanent upgrades
- Come back sharper, faster, and more dangerous

## The Breach Keeps Growing

Your rig starts small, but the upgrade trees open up quickly: more damage, faster attacks, more cargo, better fuel, wider range, drones, payout scaling, survival tools, and core-focused upgrades that help you crack deeper layers.

The firewall keeps its own pressure. Daemon cores fight back, deeper layers hit harder, and the center will not open just because you brought a bigger laser. Learn the route, tune the build, and make every extraction cleaner than the last.

## Demo Goals

- Defeat the demo breach
- Improve your gameplay-time clear
- Chase the Defeat Demo leaderboard
- Keep upgrading after messy runs
- Learn the firewall before the full breach opens

## In The Full Data Breach Inc. Structure

Incremental games, arcade mining, action roguelite pacing, upgrade trees, score chasing, cyberpunk-ish sabotage, and runs where "one more upgrade" becomes three more runs.
