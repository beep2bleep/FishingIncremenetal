# Data Breach Inc.

Pilot a breach rig into hostile corporate infrastructure, mine the firewall for resources, crack daemon cores, and get out before the system decides you are part of the cleanup.

Data Breach Inc. is an arcade incremental built around short sorties and long-term upgrades. Each run gives you a chance to mine blocks, break defenses, bank resources, and push a little deeper into the machine.

## What You Do

- Fly into the firewall
- Mine blocks and collect resources
- Fight defensive nodes and daemon cores
- Extract before the run collapses
- Spend the haul on permanent upgrades
- Unlock connected side operations
- Come back sharper, faster, and more dangerous

## The Breach Keeps Growing

Your rig starts small, but the upgrade trees get loud quickly: more damage, more cargo, better fuel, wider range, drones, payout scaling, survival tools, and core-focused upgrades that help you crack the deeper layers.

The firewall keeps its own pressure. Daemon cores fight back, countermeasures trigger, and the center will not open just because you brought a bigger laser.

## Included In The Data Breach Inc. Structure

- Open Pit style breach runs through a persistent firewall
- Red Sky Defense side operation
- Deepcore side operation
- Sortie history and results screens
- Demo goals and challenge routes
- Leaderboard-ready clear targets

## Good Fit If You Like

Incremental games, arcade mining, action roguelite pacing, upgrade trees, score chasing, cyberpunk-ish sabotage, and runs where "one more upgrade" becomes three more runs.
