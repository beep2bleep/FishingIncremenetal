# Reel Into Darkness

Fish the dark. Sell the catch. Climb the tree.

Reel Into Darkness is a **haunted fishing incremental**. You work the water for catches, sell them into currency, and feed upgrades that let you fish deeper and chase rarer species. The presentation stays moody and atmospheric—progression carries the long game.

## Why play
- Hands-on fishing with incremental payoff
- Depth and species variety as natural milestones
- Upgrade tree gives constant next goals
- Fits players who like eerie tone without high-action combat

## Core loop
1. Cast and fight fish with your current stats.
2. Land and sell catches.
3. Buy upgrades.
4. Unlock deeper bands and new targets.
5. Repeat with a stronger profile.
