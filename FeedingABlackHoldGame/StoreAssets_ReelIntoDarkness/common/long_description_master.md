# Reel Into Darkness

Fish the dark. Sell the catch. Grow strong enough to go deeper.

Reel Into Darkness is a **haunted fishing incremental**. You cast into shadowed water, manage tension and stamina as fish fight the line, land catches, and sell them for currency. Spending that currency on upgrades changes what depths you can reach, what species you can reasonably fight, and how efficient each outing becomes.

## Why play
- Combines incremental progression with a hands-on fishing loop
- Depth and species variety create reasons to push further
- Moody presentation for players who like low-key horror atmosphere
- Long upgrade tail for optimization-minded solo players

## Core loop
1. Fish at a chosen depth with your current gear stats.
2. Land catches and manage stamina/value tradeoffs on the line.
3. Sell fish to grow your wallet.
4. Buy upgrades on the tree—depth, capability, quality of life, and power.
5. Return to the water with a stronger profile and new targets.

## Gameplay systems (align with the build)
- Fish catalog with depth bands and difficulty
- Cast / reel interaction and fish behavior
- Economy: sell values and upgrade costs
- Progression through an upgrade tree specific to the mode

## Session style
Works as relaxed sessions with steady dopamine from upgrades, or longer optimization runs when chasing rare fish or depth milestones.
