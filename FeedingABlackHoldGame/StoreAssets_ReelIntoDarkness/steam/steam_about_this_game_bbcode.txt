[h1]Reel Into Darkness[/h1]

Fish the dark. Sell the catch. Feed the upgrades.

Reel Into Darkness is a haunted fishing incremental. Cast into shadowed water, manage the fight on your line, sell what you land, and invest returns into upgrades that push you toward deeper, stranger catches.

[h2]What You Do[/h2]
[list]
[*]Fish at increasing depths with your current stats
[*]Land catches and balance stamina, risk, and reward on the line
[*]Sell fish to grow your wallet
[*]Buy upgrades that expand depth access, power, and efficiency
[*]Chase rare species and long-term tree milestones
[/list]

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Hands-on fishing loop with incremental progression
[*]Species variety tied to depth and progression
[*]Upgrade tree specific to the mode’s economy
[*]Moody, atmospheric presentation
[*]Solo-friendly pacing for relaxed or optimization-focused sessions
[/list]
