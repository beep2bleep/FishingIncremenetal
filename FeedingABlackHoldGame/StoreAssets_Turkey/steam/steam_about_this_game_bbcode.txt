[h1]Turkey[/h1]

Lane play. Pin chaos. Score chasing.

Turkey is an arcade bowling-style mode built around shot skill. Line up your throw, control power and spin, and watch pins scatter with physics-driven reactions. Clear frames, chase high scores, and use meta progression to push further each session.

[h2]What You Do[/h2]
[list]
[*]Set up shots with position, power, and spin
[*]Release into a 3D lane and read pin motion
[*]Score frames and chase strikes, spares, and streaks
[*]Review summaries and invest in meta upgrades between runs
[/list]

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Readable arcade pacing with short rounds
[*]Physics-driven pin reactions
[*]Score-focused feedback loop
[*]Meta progression hooks for repeat sessions
[*]Single-player experience tuned for quick play
[/list]
