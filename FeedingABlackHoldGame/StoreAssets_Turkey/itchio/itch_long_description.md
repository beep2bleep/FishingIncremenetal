# Turkey

Lane play, pin physics, and score chasing.

Turkey is an **arcade bowling-style mode**: you line up shots, tune power and spin, and send the ball into a 3D pin deck. The fun is in readable physics feedback and incremental mastery—learning how small changes rewrite the rack.

## Why play
- Short rounds with immediate results
- Skill expression through shot setup
- Meta hooks that reward coming back for better lines

## Core loop
1. Configure your shot and release.
2. Watch the lane resolve with physics-driven pins.
3. Score the outcome and read feedback.
4. Spend progression between sessions.
5. Chase cleaner execution and higher totals.
