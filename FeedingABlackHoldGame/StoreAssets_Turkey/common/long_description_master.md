# Turkey

Lane play, pin physics, and score chasing in a compact arcade package.

Turkey is a **bowling-inspired arcade mode**: you control shot setup—where you stand, how hard you throw, and how the ball curves—then let the lane physics play out. Strikes and spares feel punchy because the pins move like physical objects, and the loop rewards learning the lane rather than memorizing abstract patterns.

## Why play
- Fast rounds that still reward skill growth
- Clear feedback on power, spin, and results
- Meta progression that gives reasons to rematch your best runs
- Fits short sessions and “one more game” momentum

## Core loop
1. Set start position and aim parameters for the shot.
2. Commit power and spin; release into the lane.
3. Watch pins settle; score the frame with readable results.
4. Review summaries and earnings or progression markers.
5. Upgrade or adjust strategy; chase a cleaner line or higher totals.
