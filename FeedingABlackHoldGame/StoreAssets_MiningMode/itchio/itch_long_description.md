# Deepcore

Drive the rig. Bring the ore home. Build the next better dive.

Deepcore is a run-based mining incremental built around short, high-pressure dives. You steer a drill rig through destructible underground terrain, break open ore nodes, pull the loose haul back toward the base ring, and cash out before the run ends from time, drill damage, or cargo pressure.

## Why Play
- Fast runs with visible upgrade payoff
- Constant pressure from timer, drill health, and cargo limits
- Deeper tiers unlock richer materials and stronger payouts
- Permanent rig upgrades and drones create steady long-term growth
- Built for repeat dives, route learning, and optimization

## Core Loop
1. Launch into the selected depth tier with your current rig.
2. Carve tunnels, break nodes, and collect the ore you shake loose.
3. Decide when to keep pushing and when to return to base.
4. Bank money and XP, unlock deeper tiers, and buy upgrades.
5. Start the next dive with better stats, better support, and a new target.

## Gameplay Features
- Mouse-steered drill-rig movement through destructible terrain
- Short runs where timer, drill wear, and cargo all matter
- Permanent upgrades for engine tuning, drill torque, plating, cooling, cargo, ore value, XP, and route efficiency
- Depth progression that steadily raises reward, resistance, and risk
- Salvage drones and delivery drones that support faster and cleaner extraction
- End-of-run summaries that highlight payout, XP, and performance

## Session Style
Deepcore works well in short bursts because runs resolve quickly, but the campaign still has a long progression tail through upgrades, depth unlocks, and loadout improvement.

## Controls
- Mouse: steer the rig and navigate menus
- The focus is route planning, haul timing, and upgrade choices rather than combat execution
