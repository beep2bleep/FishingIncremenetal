# Mining Mode

Drive the drill. Bank the ore. Build the next better run.

Mining Mode is a progression-heavy mining incremental built around short, high-pressure dives. You pilot a drill rig through underground layers, carve up ore nodes, haul resources back to base, and invest the payout into permanent upgrades that let the next dive go deeper and pay harder.

## Why Play
- Strong run-to-run momentum with visible machine upgrades
- Real tension from drill wear, cargo limits, and time pressure
- Deeper layers unlock richer materials and bigger payouts
- Automation upgrades add satisfying industrial scaling
- Built for repeat sessions and optimization-minded play

## Core Loop
1. Launch a dive and steer the drill rig into the current depth.
2. Break nodes, collect ore, and manage drill health, timer, and cargo.
3. Return to base and bank your haul before the run ends.
4. Gain money and XP, unlock deeper strata, and buy upgrades.
5. Dive again with a stronger rig, better logistics, and a bigger target.

## Gameplay Features
- Direct-control mining with destructible underground nodes
- Persistent upgrades for speed, torque, plating, cooling, cargo, value, and XP
- Depth progression that expands the ore table and raises the stakes
- Support automation through salvage drones, delivery drones, foreman bots, and sorters
- Run-end summary screens that make progress and efficiency easy to read
- Single-player progression loop designed for repeat dives

## Session Style
Works in short bursts or longer progression sessions. Runs are compact, but the meta-upgrade curve gives the mode long-tail growth.

## Controls
- Mouse: steer the drill and navigate the upgrade and summary screens
- Gameplay focus is route choice, haul timing, and upgrade planning rather than twitch combat
