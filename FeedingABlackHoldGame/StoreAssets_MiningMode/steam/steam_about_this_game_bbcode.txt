[h1]Mining Mode[/h1]

Drive the drill. Bank the ore. Build the next better run.

Mining Mode is a progression-first mining incremental built around short, high-pressure dives. Pilot a drill rig through underground layers, break ore nodes, haul the ore back to base, and turn every run into permanent upgrades that push the next run deeper.

[h2]What You Do[/h2]
[list]
[*]Steer a drill rig through destructible underground routes
[*]Manage timer drain, drill wear, and cargo pressure during each run
[*]Collect ore and bank it for money and XP
[*]Unlock deeper strata with richer materials and higher risk
[*]Invest payouts into permanent mining, logistics, and automation upgrades
[/list]

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Direct-control mining loop with strong run-to-run momentum
[*]Persistent upgrades for speed, torque, plating, cooling, cargo, ore value, and XP
[*]Depth progression that expands the material pool and payout ceiling
[*]Support automation through salvage drones, delivery drones, foreman bots, and sorters
[*]Readable summary screens that highlight depth, payout, XP, and efficiency
[*]Single-player progression built for repeat dives and optimization
[/list]

[h2]Why It Feels Good[/h2]
Every improvement is visible. The drill moves faster, the cargo holds longer, the payouts hit harder, and deeper layers stop feeling impossible. Each dive gives you a new way to push the machine further.

[list]
[*]Mining and drilling games
[*]Incremental and progression loops
[*]Optimization-heavy single-player systems
[*]Risk-versus-reward runs with persistent upgrades
[/list]
