Deepcore is a run-based mining incremental.

You steer a drill rig through underground terrain, break open ore nodes, collect the loose haul, and bank it at the base ring before the timer expires, the drill gives out, or cargo pressure cuts the run short.

Every dive becomes permanent growth. Upgrade engine tuning, drill torque, plating, cooling, cargo, ore value, XP, route efficiency, and drone support to reach deeper tiers and bring back bigger payouts on the next run.

Features:
- Mouse-steered drilling through destructible underground terrain
- Real run pressure from timer drain, drill depletion, and cargo limits
- Permanent upgrades across speed, drilling, durability, cargo, value, XP, and efficiency
- Depth-tier progression with richer materials and stronger payout targets
- Support automation through salvage drones and delivery drones
- Single-player progression loop built for repeat dives and optimization
