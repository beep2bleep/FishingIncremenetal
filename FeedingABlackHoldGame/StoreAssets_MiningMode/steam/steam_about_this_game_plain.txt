Mining Mode is a progression-first mining incremental.

You pilot a drill rig through underground layers, break ore nodes, collect resources, and bank the haul before your timer, cargo, or drill wear cuts the run short.

Every dive becomes permanent growth. Upgrade drill power, cargo flow, ore value, XP pace, and automation support to reach richer depths and larger payouts on the next run.

Features:
- Direct-control mining with destructible underground nodes
- Real run pressure from timer drain, cargo limits, and drill wear
- Persistent upgrades across speed, torque, plating, cooling, value, and progression
- Depth progression with richer materials and stronger payout targets
- Support automation through salvage drones, delivery drones, foreman bots, and sorters
- Single-player progression loop built for repeat dives and optimization
